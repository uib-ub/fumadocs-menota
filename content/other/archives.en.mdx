# Other archives in Menota

Menota’s Corpuscle application hosts other archives.

These include a selection of around 180 charters in Norwegian. All charters up to and inclding
1310 are included in the selection, in addition to some later charters.

The application also includes a selection of 100 Norwegian runic inscriptions in the younger
Runic alphabet.

- [Archive of charters](https://clarino.uib.no/menota/catalogue/menota-diploma)
- [Archive of Runic inscriptions](https://clarino.uib.no/menota/catalogue/menota-rune)
- [Le lingue nordiche](https://clarino.uib.no/menota/catalogue/menota-lln)
