## Lexicographic resources

Several dictionaries of Medieval Nordic languages are accessible on the web. They are presently in
different formats and the interfaces are not uniform, but they do represent an extremely useful
resource:

- [Fritzner’s dictionary](https://www.dok.hf.uio.no/perl/search/search.cgi?appid=86&tabid=1275) {/* link is dead */}
  (Icelandic and Norwegian)
- [Söderwall](https://spraakbanken.gu.se/karp/?mode=soederwall) and [Schlyter](https://spraakbanken.gu.se/karp/?mode=schlyter)’s dictionaries (Swedish)
    &ndash; queries in _Fornsvensk lexikalisk databas_
- [Kalkar’s dictionary](https://www.hist.uib.no/kalkar/) (Danish)
    &ndash; scanned images of the printed dictionary (launched in 2000)
- [Kalkar’s dictionary](https://kalkarsordbog.dk/) (Danish)
    &ndash; full search capabilities (launched in 2017)

A project headed by Hans Fix, University of Greifswald, has made Walter Baetke’s
_Wörterbuch zur altnordischen Prosaliteratur_ accessible in an updated version for free usage. The
dictionary can be downloaded as a PDF file (75 MB). A fast connection is thus essential.

- [Walter Baetke’s dictionary digital](hhttps://epub.ub.uni-greifswald.de/frontdoor/deliver/index/docId/7761/file/baetke_digital.pdf)

Les også om igangværende ordboksprosjekter:

- [Ordbog over det norrøne prosasprog](https://onp.ku.dk) (Icelandic and Norwegian)
- [Gammeldansk ordbog](https://dsl.dk/diverse/sprog/ordboger-og-sprogteknologi/gammeldansk-ordbog)
    &ndash; presentation of the dictionary project
- [Gammeldansk ordbog](https://gammeldanskordbog.dk/) &ndash; beta version of the dictionary with
    search capabilities
