### Citing the handbook

The whole handbook may be cited as:

*The Menota handbook: Guidelines for the electronic encoding of Medieval Nordic primary sources*. Gen. ed. Odd Einar Haugen. Version 3.0. Bergen: Medieval Nordic Text Archive, 2019. [http://www.menota.org/handbook.xml](http://www.menota.org/handbook.xml)

A chapter of the handbook may be cited as:

Friederike Richter and Beeke Stegmann. “Initials and other illuminations.” Ch. 7 of *The Menota handbook: Guidelines for the electronic encoding of Medieval Nordic primary sources*. Gen. ed. Odd Einar Haugen. Version 3.0. Bergen: Medieval Nordic Text Archive, 2019. [http://www.menota.org/handbook.xml](http://www.menota.org/handbook.xml)
