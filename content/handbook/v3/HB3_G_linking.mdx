## G.2 Reference types**event**: a sequence of related actions at a particular time and
       place**lemma**: headword form of a word**ms**: manuscript, charter or other unique text-bearing object **person**: a human being generally believed to live or have
       lived**place**: a named location such as a settlement or geographical
      feature**thing**: a reference to something not belonging to the other
      categories, such as a mythological being or object, or a fictional person **work**: a textual work such as a saga, poem or learned text
