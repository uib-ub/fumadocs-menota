## G.2 Reference typesevent: a sequence of related actions at a particular time and
       placelemma: headword form of a wordms: manuscript, charter or other unique text-bearing object person: a human being generally believed to live or have
       livedplace: a named location such as a settlement or geographical
      featurething: a reference to something not belonging to the other
      categories, such as a mythological being or object, or a fictional person work: a textual work such as a saga, poem or learned text
